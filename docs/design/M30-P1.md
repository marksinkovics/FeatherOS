# M30 Part 1: higher descriptor and open-file limits (design)

**Scope.** This part raises the kernel's fixed limits for open files and adds `RLIMIT_NOFILE`. After it:

- A process gets **256 descriptors by default** (the soft limit).
- `setrlimit` raises the soft limit as far as **1024** (the hard limit and its ceiling).
- Both tables **grow on demand** instead of taking a fixed size: a process's descriptor table (32 slots today) and the system-wide disk-file table (64 entries today).
- **1024 distinct disk, `/tmp` and `/etc` files** can be open across the system.
- `poll` takes as many entries as the soft limit allows (64 today).
- libc gains `getrlimit` and `setrlimit` (`sys/resource.h`), and `sysconf(_SC_OPEN_MAX)` reports the soft limit.
- The shell gains `ulimit -n`.
- At the limits:
  - a full process gives `EMFILE`;
  - a full system-wide disk table gives a new `ENFILE` (it gives `EMFILE` today).

**Done when:** `./build.sh unit-test`, `bash tools/quality.sh check`, `tools/check.sh --arch both --modes "SHELL STORAGE"`, then `tools/check.sh --arch both --matrix`.

## Decisions (confirmed by the user, 2026-11-19)

- **D1. The descriptor table grows on demand.**
  - It is a heap array that starts at 32 slots the first time something is put in it, then doubles: 64, 128, 256, 512, 1024.
  - It grows only up to the soft limit in force, and never past 1024.
  - It does not shrink while descriptors are open. `CloseAll` frees it.
  - Memory, at 16 B per slot:

    | Layout | Per process |
    |---|---|
    | Today | 512 B |
    | Fixed 256 | 4 KiB |
    | Fixed 1024 | 16 KiB |
    | Growable | 512 B; 4 KiB at 256; 16 KiB at 1024, and only for processes that need it |

  - While the table grows, the old and new arrays exist side by side for a moment.
  - The kernel heap never unmaps pages, so a fixed table would be a permanent cost for every process.
- **D2. The disk-file table grows too.**
  - `disk_files` becomes a heap array of `DiskFileUse` (24 B each) that starts at 64 entries and doubles up to `MaximumOpenDiskFiles = 1024`.
  - A new distinct file past 1024 gives `ENFILE`.
  - Growth that fails gives `ENOMEM`, with nothing created (I8).
  - It never shrinks, since the heap does not unmap.
  - An entry is one *distinct* file: readers of the same file share an entry, as today.
  - `FindUse` stays a linear scan under the filesystem lock.
- **D3. The limits live in the descriptor table** (`soft_limit`, `hard_limit`).
  - They are per process: all threads of a process share them.
  - `Inherit` copies them, so a spawned process inherits its parent's limits.
  - `init`, through `OpenConsole`, starts with {256, 1024}.
- **D4. Soft and hard limits follow Linux as if every process were root**, since there are no users.
  - `soft ≤ hard ≤ 1024`, and either limit can be lowered.
  - The hard limit can be raised again up to 1024. Above 1024 is `EPERM`, `RLIM_INFINITY` included.
  - `soft > hard` is `EINVAL`. A soft limit of 0 is allowed.
  - Lowering the soft limit closes nothing: descriptors at or above it keep working, but no new descriptor gets a number at or above it.
- **D5. Syscall 72 `ResourceLimit(resource, new_limits, old_limits)`** works like `prlimit` on the caller.
  - It stores the old limits first, then applies the new ones. Either pointer may be null.
  - The only resource is `ResourceOpenFiles = 7` (`RLIMIT_NOFILE`). Any other resource, `RLIMIT_STACK` and the rest included, gives `EINVAL`. A bad pointer gives `EFAULT`.
  - `getrlimit` and `setrlimit` are thin wrappers.
- **D6. `poll`.**
  - `count > soft limit` gives `EINVAL`, as on Linux.
  - The entries go into a heap buffer of `count × 8` B (at most 8 KiB), not the 16 KiB kernel stack, and it is freed on every return.
  - An allocation failure gives `ENOMEM`. `count == 0` allocates nothing.
  - `User::MaximumPollDescriptors` goes away.
- **D7. Errno at the limits, as Linux.**
  - `dup2`/`DuplicateExact` with a target at or above the soft limit gives `EBADF` (today `EINVAL`).
  - `F_DUPFD` with a minimum at or above the soft limit gives `EINVAL`.
  - No free slot below the soft limit gives `EMFILE`.
  - Failed growth of either table gives `ENOMEM`.
  - A new distinct tracked file while 1024 are open gives `ENFILE`.
- **D8. `OPEN_MAX` leaves `limits.h`**, as in glibc; programs ask `sysconf(_SC_OPEN_MAX)`. `FOPEN_MAX` (19 stdio streams) is unchanged.
- **D9. The shell's `ulimit` builtin.**
  - `ulimit -n` prints the soft limit; `ulimit -n N` sets it.
  - `ulimit -Hn` prints the hard limit; `ulimit -Hn N` sets it.
  - Errors print `ulimit: <strerror>`. Other options print the usage line.

## 0. What depends on the limits today

- **`src/user/syscall_abi.h`.** `MaximumDescriptors = 32` sizes `Files::Table::slots`. It is used in:
  - `files.cpp`: `FindSlot`, `FreeSlotLocked`, `Inherit`, `KindOf`, `Duplicate`;
  - `sysconf(_SC_OPEN_MAX)` (`unistd.cpp`);
  - `filetest.cpp`;
  - `filesystem_tests.cpp`.

  `MaximumPollDescriptors = 64` is used in `PollSyscall` (`process.cpp`, a stack array).
- **`files.cpp`.** `MaximumOpenDiskFiles = 64` is checked in `AddUse` (which gives `SyscallErrorTooManyOpenFiles`). It is scanned by `FindUse` and by `Remount`'s open-file check.
- **Spawn** is the only way to create a process; there is no fork or exec.
  - It calls `Files::Inherit`, which skips close-on-exec descriptors, or `OpenConsole`. Both return void today.
  - `CloseAll` runs at exit and when a spawn fails.
  - `ProcessRecord` comes from `AllocateZeroed`.
- **Pipes, sockets, ptys and `accept`** all reserve a slot through `FreeSlotLocked`.
- **Not affected:** there is no `select`/`FD_SETSIZE` and no `/proc/<pid>/fd`, and the shell's redirections only target 0–2.
- **Missing or stale today:**
  - `ENFILE` does not exist.
  - `libctest` expects `sysconf(_SC_OPEN_MAX) == 32`.
  - `docs/posix.md`'s limits table says 16 descriptors.

## 1. Files, types, signatures

### 1.1 ABI: `src/user/syscall_abi.h`, `src/user/syscall.h`

```cpp
ResourceLimit = 72,    // (resource, new_limits, old_limits) -> 0; either pointer may be null
constexpr uint64_t DefaultDescriptorLimit = 256;   // a new session's soft RLIMIT_NOFILE
constexpr uint64_t MaximumDescriptorLimit = 1024;  // the default hard limit and its ceiling
constexpr uint64_t ResourceOpenFiles = 7;          // RLIMIT_NOFILE
struct ResourceLimits { uint64_t current; uint64_t maximum; };   // laid out as struct rlimit
constexpr uint64_t SyscallErrorTooManyFilesInSystem = UINT64_MAX - 52;  // ENFILE (check the free value)
inline uint64_t ResourceLimit(uint64_t resource, const ResourceLimits* requested, ResourceLimits* previous);
```

`MaximumDescriptors` and `MaximumPollDescriptors` are removed.

### 1.2 `src/kernel/files.{h,cpp}`

```cpp
struct Table {
    Table() = default; Table(const Table&) = delete; Table& operator=(const Table&) = delete;
    Slot* slots = nullptr;     // capacity entries on the heap, or null: no descriptors
    uint32_t capacity = 0;
    uint32_t soft_limit = User::DefaultDescriptorLimit;
    uint32_t hard_limit = User::MaximumDescriptorLimit;
};
bool OpenConsole(Table* table, Terminals::Terminal* console);       // false: out of memory
bool Inherit(Table* child, const Table* parent);                    // false: out of memory, child empty
uint64_t DescriptorLimits(Table* table, const User::ResourceLimits* requested,
                          User::ResourceLimits* previous);           // 0, EINVAL, EPERM
```

**The descriptor table:**

- `FindSlot` and `FindFile` gain `REQUIRES(file_system_lock)` and are bounded by `capacity`.
- `FreeSlotLocked(table, minimum)`:
  1. Checks `minimum < soft_limit`.
  2. Scans `[minimum, min(capacity, soft))`.
  3. If nothing is free, calls `GrowLocked(table, needed)`, which rounds up to a power of two ≥ 32 and caps at the soft limit.
  4. Returns an index, `EMFILE` or `NoMemory`.
- `Duplicate`'s exact path calls `GrowLocked(table, target + 1)`.
- `Inherit`:
  - sizes the child to `max(32, pow2(highest inherited + 1))`;
  - allocates first, then takes references (I5).
- `CloseAll` frees `slots` and sets `capacity` to 0.

**The disk-file table:**

- `disk_files` becomes `DiskFileUse* disk_files` with `disk_file_capacity`, both guarded by `file_system_lock`.
- `AddUse` runs in this order:
  1. Find the key: a hit adds a use.
  2. On a miss with `disk_file_count == 1024`: `SyscallErrorTooManyFilesInSystem`.
  3. On a miss with the table full below 1024: `GrowDiskFilesLocked` doubles it (64 → 1024) and copies the entries. A failed allocation gives `NoMemory`.
  4. Append the entry.
- No `DiskFileUse*` or `&` is held across a call that can grow the table; callers keep indices or re-find (I10).
- In `Open`, when `OpenCreate` is set, a full table (1024, or growth impossible) fails before `VFS::Create`: check, or grow ahead, while still holding the lock (I8).

### 1.3 `src/kernel/process.cpp`, `syscall.{h,cpp}`

- `Spawn` gives `NoResources` when `Inherit` or `OpenConsole` fails.
- `PollSyscall` reads its cap with `DescriptorLimits` and uses a heap buffer (D6).
- New `ResourceLimitSyscall`:
  1. Check the resource.
  2. Copy the requested limits in (`EFAULT` on a bad pointer).
  3. Call `Files::DescriptorLimits`.
  4. Copy the old limits out.
- `Handlers::resource_limit` and its dispatch in `syscall.cpp`.

## 2. Invariants

- **I1.** Every read or write of `Table::slots`, `capacity` or the limits, and of `disk_files`/`disk_file_capacity`/`disk_file_count`, happens under `file_system_lock`.
- **I2.** No `Slot*`/`Slot&` is held across a call that can grow the descriptor table; callers keep indices. Only `FreeSlotLocked`/`GrowLocked`/`Duplicate` grow it. `CreatePipe` reserves twice and keeps the reader as an index. **Riskiest.**
- **I3.** Lookups are bounded by `capacity`; new numbers are bounded by `soft_limit`.
- **I4.** `0 ≤ soft ≤ hard ≤ 1024` and `capacity ≤ 1024` at all times.
- **I5.** `Inherit` is all-or-nothing: if the allocation fails, no references are taken and the child is empty. The child's limits equal the parent's.
- **I6.** `CloseAll` leaves `{slots = null, capacity = 0}`. Every process table is set up by `OpenConsole` or `Inherit` before user code runs (`ProcessRecord` is zero-filled, so its limits are {0, 0} until then).
- **I7.** `disk_file_count ≤ disk_file_capacity ≤ 1024`. `ENFILE` happens only when a new key is needed. Every scan covers `[0, disk_file_count)`.
- **I8.** An open that fails with `EMFILE`, `ENFILE` or `ENOMEM` creates nothing.
- **I9.** No kernel stack buffer scales with a limit. `poll`'s buffer is freed on every path (ready, timeout, `EINTR`, `EFAULT`).
- **I10.** No `DiskFileUse*`/`&` is held across `AddUse` or `GrowDiskFilesLocked`. Growth copies every entry and frees the old array. **Riskiest with I2.**

## 3. Edge cases and errno

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| `open` with all of 0..soft−1 taken | `EMFILE`, nothing created |
| Growing either table fails | `ENOMEM`, nothing created |
| `dup2(fd, soft)` or above | `EBADF` |
| `fcntl(F_DUPFD, soft)` | `EINVAL` |
| `dup2(fd, 900)` with soft 1024 and capacity 32 | grows to 1024 and gives 900 |
| `setrlimit` with soft > hard | `EINVAL` |
| `setrlimit` with hard > 1024 (`RLIM_INFINITY` included) | `EPERM` |
| `setrlimit` lowering hard, then raising it again ≤ 1024 | 0 |
| Soft lowered below open descriptors | they still read and close; `open` gives `EMFILE` |
| `poll` with count > soft | `EINVAL`, nothing copied |
| 1025th distinct tracked file | `ENFILE` |
| Another reader of an already-open file at 1024 | succeeds |
| `O_CREAT` of a new file at 1024 | `ENFILE`, the file is not created |
| `Spawn` whose program file cannot be tracked | `NoResources` |
| `getrlimit(99, …)` or `RLIMIT_STACK` | `EINVAL` |
| bad `rlimit*` | `EFAULT` |
| `ulimit -n 2000` | `ulimit: Operation not permitted` |

## 5. Tests

### Host (`tests/unit/kernel/filesystem/filesystem_tests.cpp`, `virtual_files_tests.cpp`, `syscall/syscall_tests.cpp`)

- **T1. The table grows to the soft limit.**
  1. `OpenConsole`, then open `/BLOCK.TXT` 253 times: 3..255 in order.
  2. The next open gives `EMFILE`, and `CountOpen == 256`.
  3. Close 100, then open: 100.
  4. `CloseAll`, then `Close(0)` gives `EBADF`.
- **T2. Bounds and errno.**
  - `Duplicate(f, 200, 0)` gives 200.
  - `Duplicate(f, 255, Exact)` gives 255; with 256 it gives `EBADF`.
  - `Duplicate(f, 256, 0)` gives `EINVAL`.
  - `Close(5000)` gives `EBADF`, and `KindOf(1ULL << 40)` gives `None`.
- **T3. Limits.**
  1. Reading them gives {256, 1024}.
  2. Open up to 50, then set {40, 1024}: 41..50 still read and close, and a new open gives `EMFILE`.
  3. With {300, 1024}, opens reach 299.
  4. {500, 400} gives `EINVAL`, {10, 2000} gives `EPERM`, and {10, 100} then {10, 1024} gives 0.
- **T4. Inherit.**
  1. The parent has 3 (close-on-exec) and 200, with limits {300, 1024}.
  2. The child gets 0–2 and 200, not 3, with limits {300, 1024}.
  3. A read on the child's 200 shares the offset.
  4. `CloseAll` on both, with no ASan leak.
- **T5. `ENFILE`** (a fixture with 1025 files, two tables at {1024, 1024}).
  1. A opens 600 distinct files and B opens 424. B's next distinct open gives `ENFILE`.
  2. B opens one of A's files: it succeeds.
  3. B's `O_CREAT` of a new name gives `ENFILE`, and `Stat` gives `ENOENT`.
  4. A closes one, and B's open succeeds.
- **T5b. The disk table grows and keeps its entries.**
  1. Open 64 distinct files, then a 65th (forcing growth to 128), then reach 129 (growing to 256).
  2. Every earlier file still reads at its offset, and `Remount`'s busy check sees all of them.
  3. When the kernel heap has a failure hook for tests, a failed growth gives `ENOMEM` with nothing created. Otherwise, say so in the report.
- **T6. The whole disk table is scanned.**
  1. 70 distinct files are open on `/`, plus a `/tmp` file open for writing as the 71st.
  2. `Remount("/tmp", ReadOnly)` gives `EBUSY`.
  3. After closing it, the remount gives 0.
- **T7. Dispatch.** `ResourceLimit(7, a, b)` reaches the handler with all three arguments.

### QEMU (`src/user/libctest.cpp`, the SHELL case, both architectures)

- **T8. Defaults.** `getrlimit(RLIMIT_NOFILE)` gives {256, 1024}, and `sysconf(_SC_OPEN_MAX) == 256`.
- **T9. Past the old limits.**
  1. Open 80 distinct files.
  2. `dup` until `EMFILE`: the last is 255.
  3. After `setrlimit` {300, 1024}, `dup` reaches 299.
  4. `dup2(fd, 300)` gives `EBADF`; {2000, 2000} gives `EPERM`; {400, 300} gives `EINVAL`.

## 6. Risks for the implementer

- **I2 and I10 are easy to break** while editing `Open`, `CreatePipe`, `AddTerminalLocked` and `AddUse`'s callers. Reserve first, then take references, and never grow after taking one.
- **Host tests that build a `Table` without calling `CloseAll`** now leak under LSan. About 54 places in six files need checking.
- **The kernel heap never unmaps**, so freed tables are reused, not returned.
