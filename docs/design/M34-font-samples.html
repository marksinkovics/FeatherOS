FeatherOS Font Pairs

The same sample in each candidate pair: the console strip is the monospace face at the 8x16 cell size, below it the UI face at 16, 32 and 48 px, with Hungarian accents, Greek, Cyrillic and box drawing. In FeatherOS the glyphs would be pre-rendered with 4-bit anti-aliasing, so they will look very close to this, slightly softer at 16 px.

Are they free to use? Yes. All four pairs are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1: free to use, embed, convert and redistribute, also commercially, as long as the licence text travels with the fonts, the fonts are not sold on their own, and a converted version does not carry a reserved font name. FeatherOS would name its files mono-N and sans-N and ship each family's licence beside them in /system/fonts/.
